1. Executive Summary & Core Concept

CloakHer is a proactive web and mobile ecosystem engineered to protect women's social media photographs from unauthorized AI exploitation, including non-consensual deepfakes, automated "nudify" algorithms, and extortion schemes.

Unlike conventional safety apps that react only after an attack or leak occurs, CloakHer immunizes digital photos prior to public broadcast. By injecting imperceptible adversarial perturbations and frequency-domain cryptographic watermarks into facial and anatomical pixels, CloakHer ensures photos look perfectly natural to human viewers while rendering them unprocessable and mathematically disruptive to generative AI models.

2. Problem Identification & Prioritization

Validated Problem Statement (Session 01 Standard):
"Young women and female university students active on digital media (Who) struggle with unauthorized scraping and weaponization of their personal photographs into AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery and blackmail material (What) because publicly posted photographs lack pre-emptive technical immunization against generative diffusion models and victims possess no instantaneous method to legally prove synthetic tampering (Why), leading to severe psychological distress, reputational extortion, academic derailment, and forced withdrawal from digital society (Consequence)."

Why Traditional Watermarks Fail

Visible watermarks ruin personal photographs and can be easily cropped or removed using standard inpainting tools. Furthermore, unregulated deepfake engines ignore visual copyright marks entirely.

The Proactive Protection Imperative

Once a forged image is leaked, legal remediation (e.g., reporting to CERT or police) is slow and reactive. The psychological and social damage has already happened. The only definitive solution is pre-publication immunity.

3. Technical Architecture: The 3-Layer Defense Engine

CloakHer does not rely on visual overlays. It utilizes mathematical defenses embedded directly into the pixel tensor:

Layer Technology Function & Impact
Layer 1 Adversarial Latent Disruption
(Targeted PGD Perturbations)		 Alters pixel gradients by infinitesimal values (±1-2 RGB). When ingested by diffusion models or latent encoders, it induces mathematical collapse, producing distorted, scrambled artifacts instead of recognizable faces.
Layer 2 C2PA + DWT-DCT Steganography
(Frequency Provenance)		 Embeds tamper-resilient metadata and frequency-domain watermarks surviving compression and screenshots. Compliant commercial AI APIs recognize the "Protected Identity" flag and automatically abort synthesis.
Layer 3 Zero-Knowledge Perceptual Hashing
(Post-Incident Triage)		 Calculates one-way perceptual hashes (pHash) without retaining raw user photos. If deepfake extortion occurs, the user uploads the counterfeit to instantly generate a CERT-admissible forensic verification report.

4. Agentic AI Workflow (Session 02 Alignment)

CloakHer leverages an autonomous Plan → Act → Observe → Decide agentic execution loop as formulated in the Artemia 2.0 technical workshops:

  • 1. PLAN: The agent detects facial landmarks, skin textures, and lighting vectors to calculate the minimum adversarial perturbation required to disrupt generative models while preserving 99.8% human visual fidelity.
  • 2. ACT: Tools apply the mathematical perturbation matrix and write the cryptographic steganographic signature into the Discrete Wavelet Transform (DWT) domain.
  • 3. OBSERVE (Autonomous Self-Test): The agent immediately routes the cloaked image through a sandboxed internal diffusion encoder to test whether the synthetic representation can be reconstructed.
  • 4. DECIDE: If facial feature embeddings remain recognizable above threshold, the agent dynamically recalibrates perturbation intensity until immunization achieves >95% confidence, subsequently releasing the verified asset for user download.
Responsible Autonomy & Guardrails:
  • Zero-Storage Architecture (Blast Radius Limitation): Raw uncloaked photographs reside purely in transient volatile memory (RAM) and are purged immediately after processing.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Safeguard: Forensic dossiers and CERT complaint forms require explicit user review and digital signature before transmission.
